# Design notes v0.1

_Starting direction only. We'll design properly before building screens._

## Feeling
- **Playful, bold, a little cheeky.** Think game night, not productivity app.
- The **spin / reveal** moment is the heart of the app. It should feel like opening a card or rolling dice: a short animation, a bit of suspense.
- Photos of completed challenges are the reward, so the timeline should look like a scrapbook, not a list.

## Visual direction (to explore)
- Dark background with one loud accent color (e.g. electric orange or lime) for "Spin" and points.
- Big, chunky rounded type for challenge titles.
- Category icons/colors: 🥾 Outdoors · 🍳 Food · 🎨 Creative · 🧠 Learn · 🎲 Games · 🤝 Social · 🔥 Spicy · 🏠 Quick wins

## v1 screens
1. **Welcome / login**
2. **Start, find or join a crew, or go solo** (first stop after sign-up; "Just me for now" skips the crew)
3. **Home:** big Spin button + filters (group size, time, budget, in/out)
4. **Challenge reveal:** title, details, points, "Let's do it" / one re-roll
5. **Active challenge:** what we're doing now + "Done! Add proof"
6. **Upload proof:** photo + one-line note
7. **Group timeline:** scrapbook of completed challenges
8. **Leaderboard:** points per person
9. **Account:** name, avatar color, password, language, sign out (bottom tab, added 2026-10-19)
10. **Crews:** bottom tab between Leaderboard and Account. Just me, your crews, requests you're waiting on, search by name, start a crew, paste a link. A dot shows when someone wants into a crew you run (added 2026-10-22)
11. **Crew page:** members (the admin wears a crown), join requests for the admin, invite link, "Spin with this crew", leave crew (added 2026-10-22)

## Mobile first
Almost everyone will use it on a phone. Design for phone screens first; desktop is a bonus.
